# Fight designs — more fighting sense

Static review of five existing animation-pack duels. No new paintings. Each design is the shot standoff plus the six fight beats already under `assets/animation/fight/`.

## The five designs

1. **Gate Vanguard Clash** — `ep1-vanguard-s05`
2. **Warden Staff Break** — `ep1-warden-s05`
3. **Heron Silk vs Staff** — `ep1-heron-s04`
4. **Lu Bu Terrace Duel** — `ep1-lubu-s03`
5. **Cloud Bridge Finale** — `ep2-final-s01`, with a short flash of `insert/ep1-final-duel-insert-four-weapons.png` on impact

Beat order: standoff (the matching `shot/` plate), wind-up, charge, impact, pass, hold, aftermath.
