# Fight designs — v1 animatic

Directed camera on the existing animation-pack plates. No new paintings and no cutouts. V2 cutouts are a separate Art Director pass.

Zhao Yun’s weapon in the copy is a **jian**. The camera only crops and cuts. It does not redraw a spear, staff, or any other baked pixel.

## Grammar

Each fight, same compositor:

1. **wind-up** — wide ¾ hold on `{id}-windup.png`
2. **charge** — short push-in tracking the attacker on `{id}-charge.png`
3. **impact** — hard cut to a tip-clash close-up of `{id}-impact.png`, with the existing shake, white flash, and speed lines
4. **aftermath** — pull out to a medium-wide hold on `{id}-aftermath.png`

If the shot plate exists, play opens on a wide standoff. Pass and hold plates flash briefly between impact and aftermath.
